### NFL picks (each recorded at first sight and at kickoff, graded, its own Models row)

- **Spread PICK side**, in order: a tested rule — LINE MOVE (books moved across 3 / 7 or 3+ points, Polymarket still on the old number: 63.5% / 60.3%), BOUNCE BACK (road team missed by 24+: 61.3% of 261), BOUNCE BACK DOG (scored 3 or less, now the dog: 56.5%), INJURY (one team's QB / 2+ OL / 2+ DB NEWLY out, line not yet moved 1+: QB 66.7% of 51, OL/DB 53.7% of 95; weeks 4+), TEAM MODEL (team-numbers fit 58%+ sure: 63.4% of 257; moved 1+ against it 70.6%, moved toward it = no pick), OVERSHOOT (game model 2+ points off the line AND the line moved 1+ toward it -> the other side: 57.0%) — else the STACK model's side (52.8% / 56.2% confident) when its inputs are complete, else the game model's side (50.6%, yellow). BEAT THE tiers (books 1 / 1.5 / 2.5 -> dog +3.5, 3.5 -> dog +5.5, 5.5 -> dog +7.5) are recorded and graded but never name the side.
- **Total PICK**: TOTAL MOVE (ridge predicts the move from the open: top 20% 57.8%, top 10% 60.9%; weeks 4+), else the game model's side (51.7%).
- **ML PICK**: STACK on a 50-70% favorite (67.7 / 69.7%), or TEAM MODEL 58%+ with the line moved 1+ against it (favorite 88.9% of 18, dog priced 30-50c 63.3% of 30).
- **Win % for any line**: `lib/linetable.js` (lowest of closing 1999-2025 / opening 2014-25 / recent 2020-25; spreads shrunk toward the pooled side + cushion, K 200). Rule PICKs lift their bought lines by the PICK's edge; the model-side total uses 51.7 + the smallest step.
- **1H SPREAD PICK (10/28, `lib/h1pick.js`)**: boosted-tree mean of the home first-half margin from 7 inputs (full-game home favored margin, total, wind in open stadiums, dome, division, weeks 1-4, rest difference; fit 2011-25, `lib/h1model_data.js` generated by scratchpad `gm/gen_h1.py`), chance = the 300 past games with the nearest out-of-fold prediction. Rule (10/28 evening, after the recheck): the MODEL alone picks the side and line (rule 13: price never picks), shown % = the model's chance - 11.4 points, 58%+ on that, 77c or less, steady two-sided books only (the two prices adding to 106c or less), highest per game. Why: on Polymarket US's steady books (2026 wk1-7) the model alone said 73.4 and won 62.0% (31-19); the 10/28-night blend (0.4 model + 0.6 price) said 73 and won 64%, and it let the price choose the line (any side priced in the 70s cleared 58%; 7 of 14 week-8 picks were sides the model put under their price). This rule on those books, the app's own code: 27-15 = 64.3% (shown 63.8, priced 74c, -13.6 per $100), weeks 1-3 14-7, weeks 4-7 13-8 -- green on win rate, no money edge at US prices. (International fresh closes, a different venue where the model ran LOW: 51-18 = 73.9%, said 65.7.) Saved / rewritten by the 15-min pull until kickoff (`teaser:<s>:<w>` `<game>|h1spr`), taken out when nothing qualifies except in the last hour before kickoff (`lastHour`: a blank book on the last pull must not erase a bet), graded on ESPN's quarters 1+2 (`lib/espnFinals.js firstHalvesFromScoreboard`), Models -> Card picks "1H SPREAD · PICK".
- **GAME PROPS PICK (10/28 night, `lib/gameprops.js`)**: Polymarket US game / team props (total passing / rushing / all TDs, two-point, D/ST TDs, overtime, safety, tie, first score, first TD, race to 7-35, half / full, exact margin, team TDs / field goals / D/ST TD / yards). Chance = how often the market's condition came true in the 600 games of 2011-25 nearest this game's spread + total (placed by ridge fits of the market's own quantities, `lib/gameprops_data.js` generated by scratchpad `us/gen_gp.py`). Shown % = 0.8 that + 0.2 price. Rule: shown 70%+, price 30-77c, shown 6+ points over the price, a real two-sided book (98-106c), one pick per game per market type. ~11,400 model options tried (scratchpad `us/engine2.py`); on the 2026 wk1-7 steady books (prices = best ask, checked), app code: 161-28 = 85.2% (said 81.4, 67c, +30 per $100), weeks 4-7 62-10 = 86.1%, market-price draws never reach it (`npm run accuracy`, `railway-model-service/data/poly_us_gameprops.csv`). Halves / quarters / team totals in the same run: 22-12 at a said 72% (even money) -- not picked. Saved / rewritten by the 15-min pull until kickoff (`teaser:<s>:<w>` `<game>|gp|<type>`; kept in the last hour as the 1H pick), graded on Polymarket's own settlement (outcomePrices in marketSides order), Models -> Card picks "GAME PROPS · PICK". Books are 1c bid / 40-60c ask until game day, so picks appear on game day.
- **PASSING TDs PICK (10/28 night, `lib/gameprops.js ptdCross`)**: the two QBs' passing-TD ladders (1+ .. 4+) convolved into the game's total passing TDs; when that differs from the game market "Total Passing Touchdowns Over X" by 8+ points, the game market's side the QBs point to (30-77c, real two-sided book), one per game (biggest gap). Shown % = 0.8 QB-implied + 0.2 price. App code on Polymarket US 2026 wk1-7, prices 2 h before kickoff (`railway-model-service/data/poly_us_ptd_cross.csv`, `npm run accuracy`): 56-18 = 75.7% (said 75.3, 55c, +43 per $100), weeks 4-7 32-7; market-price draws reach it 1 in 10,000. Rides the GAME PROPS pick (same recording / grading / tab, `<game>|gp|ptd_qb`, skipped when it is the very side a GAME PROPS pick holds), its own Models row "PASSING TDs · PICK". The passing-YARDS version went 24-3 but those markets stopped after week 3.

## HOW THINGS ARE TESTED

- Every new pick source: each season predicted by a fit on earlier seasons only; then on Polymarket's REAL prices (fresh closes only — stale last trades made fake edges 10/28); choose options on one period, judge on a later one; luck ceiling = the same pipeline on shuffled results / results drawn at the market's own prices. A rate measured on the games a pattern was found in is not a win rate.
- `npm test` = NFL site checks (714), grading audit, service checks, NBA checks (431); `npm run accuracy` = every rule replayed vs its card number + live records; `npm run verify` = the whole season re-graded from outside data (`-- --export=FILE` for the app's export).
- Browser checks: serve `public/` through Playwright routes with live JSON fixtures (the sandbox proxy breaks HTTPS for Chromium). Pages at 390 and 1440 px, 0 errors, nothing off the page.

## TESTED, NO EDGE (don't redo without a new idea)

- **Game model / spreads**: the closing line beats every game model tried (ridge any strength, GBM, Poisson, Elo, 538, EPA ratings, NGS, QBR, PFR, special teams, success rate, market-implied ratings, kernel line tables); situation / pair grids are noise as a whole (PBO 76%, 0 of 1,748 survive FDR); the wide all-flags model finds the card's own rules only; betting splits (MGM, Action Network) no edge; timing (bet early vs Sunday) no difference.
- **Totals**: wind / weather (post-game data cut), key totals, early-season Under, division / primetime Unders, every Over/Under form rule.
- **Polymarket US checks 10/28 evening (research)**: when to bet (prices move < 1c from 24 h out to kickoff), full-game spread / total ladders, moneyline, team totals, game total vs team totals (NFL + NBA), team TDs vs game TDs, spread vs ML, player yards vs his TD price, the TD model vs US prices, first TD, a sharper prop model (opponent defense / spread / wind / target share), NBA team totals vs players' points: no edge. NBA player-prop No sides are NOT buyable (owner); NBA game / team markets are.
- **Game markets (10/28, Polymarket US 2026 wk1-7 steady books, ~11,400 options)**: every half / quarter spread, total and moneyline, team totals, quarter / half BTS — no option holds (22-12 at a said 72%, even money). The game / team props DO hold (the GAME PROPS PICK above); the earlier one-sided-ask study (10/27) read last trades, not books.
- **TD model**: rebuilds (per position, Poisson, deeper trees, blends), opponent defense splits, NGS, recency weighting, weather, team residuals, calibration layers, a first-TD calibration — none beat the current model walk-forward.
- **Props**: NFL TD opening price as an input (worse), NFL price drift, hot / cold form, top target out (market adjusts), Unders on receptions (fail on real lines); NBA back-to-back rebounds, star-out Unders, blocks, hot / cold.
- **Moneyline**: the market's fee-out price is calibrated; no stack or model beats it except the two ML PICK rules.
