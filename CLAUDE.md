### NFL picks (each recorded at first sight and at kickoff, graded, its own Models row)

- **Spread PICK side**, in order: a tested rule — LINE MOVE (books moved across 3 / 7 or 3+ points, Polymarket still on the old number: 63.5% / 60.3%), BOUNCE BACK (road team missed by 24+: 61.3% of 261), BOUNCE BACK DOG (scored 3 or less, now the dog: 56.5%), INJURY (one team's QB / 2+ OL / 2+ DB NEWLY out, line not yet moved 1+: QB 66.7% of 51, OL/DB 53.7% of 95; weeks 4+), TEAM MODEL (team-numbers fit 58%+ sure: 63.4% of 257; moved 1+ against it 70.6%, moved toward it = no pick), OVERSHOOT (game model 2+ points off the line AND the line moved 1+ toward it -> the other side: 57.0%) — else the STACK model's side (52.8% / 56.2% confident) when its inputs are complete, else the game model's side (50.6%, yellow). BEAT THE tiers (books 1 / 1.5 / 2.5 -> dog +3.5, 3.5 -> dog +5.5, 5.5 -> dog +7.5) are recorded and graded but never name the side.
- **Total PICK**: TOTAL MOVE (ridge predicts the move from the open: top 20% 57.8%, top 10% 60.9%; weeks 4+), else the game model's side (51.7%).
- **ML PICK**: STACK on a 50-70% favorite (67.7 / 69.7%), or TEAM MODEL 58%+ with the line moved 1+ against it (favorite 88.9% of 18, dog priced 30-50c 63.3% of 30).
- **Win % for any line**: `lib/linetable.js` (lowest of closing 1999-2025 / opening 2014-25 / recent 2020-25; spreads shrunk toward the pooled side + cushion, K 200). Rule PICKs lift their bought lines by the PICK's edge; the model-side total uses 51.7 + the smallest step.
- **1H SPREAD PICK (10/28, `lib/h1pick.js`)**: boosted-tree mean of the home first-half margin from 7 inputs (full-game home favored margin, total, wind in open stadiums, dome, division, weeks 1-4, rest difference; fit 2011-25, `lib/h1model_data.js` generated by scratchpad `gm/gen_h1.py`), chance = the 300 past games with the nearest out-of-fold prediction, model alone (blends did no better). Shown % = 0.4 model + 0.6 Polymarket's price (10/28 night: on Polymarket US's own prices of 2026 wk1-7 -- the US site keeps every past game's price history -- the model alone said 76-80 and won 72-75 on its picks; the blend said 73.4 / 74.8 and won 72.7 / 75.9%, 73-25 overall, and on the international closes 47-13 = 78.3%). Rule (owner 10/28): that % 58%+ and 77c or less, the price never drops a side (the first rule copied the props' 65% from 60c + priced under its chance -- wrong, the owner set 58%), highest chance per game. Real Polymarket fresh closes (last trade within 2 h of kickoff), 2025 wk10-18 + 2026 wk1-7: 51-18 = 73.9% (said 65.7, 61c, +22 per $100), 2025 14-5, 2026 wk1-3 13-3, wk4-7 24-10 = 70.6%; market-price draws reach it 1.2%, shuffled chances 0% (scratchpad gm/rule28.py; the old rule 40-16); with forecast wind instead of measured 39-16; results drawn at the market's prices reach it 3.5%, chances shuffled 0%. It leans to underdogs (82%) with big first-half cushions. Live sees ~25 lines a game (the test only saw lines that traded near kickoff), so the live record is the judge. Saved / rewritten by the 15-min pull until kickoff (`teaser:<s>:<w>` `<game>|h1spr`), taken out when nothing qualifies, graded on ESPN's quarters 1+2 (`lib/espnFinals.js firstHalvesFromScoreboard`), Models -> Card picks "1H SPREAD · PICK". 10/28 night, on the STEADY US books only (2 h before kickoff, both sides adding to 98-106c, the same books the GAME PROPS test used): 32-18 = 64.0% (said 73.4, 74c, -12.5 per $100), weeks 4-7 14-10 -- still green on win rate, its % runs ~9 high there; kept as the owner set it, the live record judges.
- **GAME PROPS PICK (10/28 night, `lib/gameprops.js`)**: Polymarket US game / team props (total passing / rushing / all TDs, two-point, D/ST TDs, overtime, safety, tie, first score, first TD, race to 7-35, half / full, exact margin, team TDs / field goals / D/ST TD / yards). Chance = how often the market's condition came true in the 600 games of 2011-25 nearest this game's spread + total (placed by ridge fits of the market's own quantities, `lib/gameprops_data.js` generated by scratchpad `us/gen_gp.py`). Shown % = 0.8 that + 0.2 price. Rule: shown 70%+, price 30-77c, shown 6+ points over the price, a real two-sided book (98-106c), one pick per game per market type. ~11,400 model options tried (scratchpad `us/engine2.py`); on the 2026 wk1-7 steady books (prices = best ask, checked), app code: 161-28 = 85.2% (said 81.4, 67c, +30 per $100), weeks 4-7 62-10 = 86.1%, market-price draws never reach it (`npm run accuracy`, `railway-model-service/data/poly_us_gameprops.csv`). ### NFL props, TD, parlays

- **Player props (`lib/props.js`)**: shown families rec yds Under (70%+), rec yds Over (70%+), receptions Over (65%+), rush yds Under (75%+); shown % = 0.6 model + 0.4 price, then band-calibrated; priced 50-77c and under its %; one row per player + stat (highest %). Everything else is scored and graded in the background. A player with no snap = push. Re-graded twice 20+ h apart.
- **Anytime TD**: the 5 likeliest per team, red "✗ no TD last N games" note, Best TD picks = 55%+; shown % = half model, half Polymarket's mid on a real book (two-sided, 5c or less wide), else the model; TD calibration OFF (walk-forward worse at every strength); WR / TE at 45%+ shifted down (they scored ~40% in every season). First TD = team chance (spread fit) x player share.
- **Parlays**: same game 2 legs (fav ML + its top scorer 55%+), 3 legs (a 60%+ favorite ML + Over + its top scorer at 55%+, total 45+), Spread + total; Best Legs (2-3 green picks / 2-3 likeliest scorers, one per game, $10); Prop Parlays 6 / 8 / 10; no 4-leg, no Upset (cut 10/26). Payout = Polymarket's real combo quote (RFQ, never accepted, `lib/comboquote.js`) refreshed in the background.
- **Shadows (recorded + graded, never picks)**: INJURY EARLY, STACK, WIDE, TD JUMP (Models -> Tracked angles).

## HOW THINGS ARE TESTED

- Every new pick source: each season predicted by a fit on earlier seasons only; then on Polymarket's REAL prices (fresh closes only — stale last trades made fake edges 10/28); choose options on one period, judge on a later one; luck ceiling = the same pipeline on shuffled results / results drawn at the market's own prices. A rate measured on the games a pattern was found in is not a win rate.
- `npm test` = NFL site checks (663), grading audit, service checks, NBA checks (416); `npm run accuracy` = every rule replayed vs its card number + live records; `npm run verify` = the whole season re-graded from outside data (`-- --export=FILE` for the app's export).
- Browser checks: serve `public/` through Playwright routes with live JSON fixtures (the sandbox proxy breaks HTTPS for Chromium). Pages at 390 and 1440 px, 0 errors, nothing off the page.

## TESTED, NO EDGE (don't redo without a new idea)

- **Game model / spreads**: the closing line beats every game model tried (ridge any strength, GBM, Poisson, Elo, 538, EPA ratings, NGS, QBR, PFR, special teams, success rate, market-implied ratings, kernel line tables); situation / pair grids are noise as a whole (PBO 76%, 0 of 1,748 survive FDR); the wide all-flags model finds the card's own rules only; betting splits (MGM, Action Network) no edge; timing (bet early vs Sunday) no difference.
- **Totals**: wind / weather (post-game data cut), key totals, early-season Under, division / primetime Unders, every Over/Under form rule.
- **Game markets (10/28, Polymarket US 2026 wk1-7 steady books, ~11,400 options)**: every half / quarter spread, total and moneyline, team totals, quarter / half BTS — no option holds (22-12 at a said 72%, even money). The game / team props DO hold (the GAME PROPS PICK above); the earlier one-sided-ask study (10/27) read last trades, not books.
- **TD model**: rebuilds (per position, Poisson, deeper trees, blends), opponent defense splits, NGS, recency weighting, weather, team residuals, calibration layers, a first-TD calibration — none beat the current model walk-forward.
- **Props**: NFL TD opening price as an input (worse), NFL price drift, hot / cold form, top target out (market adjusts), Unders on receptions (fail on real lines); NBA back-to-back rebounds, star-out Unders, blocks, hot / cold.
- **Moneyline**: the market's fee-out price is calibrated; no stack or model beats it except the two ML PICK rules.
