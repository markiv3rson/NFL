## Where things live

- Site: `nba/lib/*`, routes `pages/api/nba/*` (every route is `/api/nba/...`), pages `public/nba/index.html` + `public/nba/app.js`, tests `nba/tests/*` (`npm run test:nba`).
- Model service (Railway): `nba/railway-model-service/` — `nba_data.py` (data + cleaning + the site's closes via `/api/nba/closes`), `nba_injuries.py`, `nba_features.py`, `fair_line.py`, `fit_model.py` (numbers + accuracy report), `live.py` (tonight's games, line table), `team_side.py` (team model, data only), `nba_prop_model.py` (props, teammates-out input v2), `season_sim.py` (futures), `daily_rebuild.py` + `scheduler.py`, `app.py`.
- Database (10/28): the paid Redis Cloud database shared with NFL (`NBA_DB_URL_REDIS_URL`), every NBA key under `nba:`; the old Upstash (`NBA_REDIS_URL`, out of requests 10/27) is no longer used (owner removing it 10/28).
- Settings (combined Vercel project): `NBA_DB_URL_REDIS_URL` (shared with NFL), `NBA_MODEL_SERVICE_URL`, `NBA_MODEL_SERVICE_TOKEN`, `NBA_ODDS_API_KEY` — NBA never reads NFL's keys (its own `nba:` ones only), service or odds key. Railway NBA service: root `/nba/railway-model-service`, config `/nba/railway-model-service/railway.json`, `SITE_URL`, `SITE_API=/api/nba`, `SITE_LOGIN`, `MODEL_DIR` + `NBA_CACHE` on a volume.
- Jobs (Pacific): rebuild 5:15 AM (ESPN's box files land ~4:30 AM), prop refit 5:35, futures 5:55, catch-up 8:15 when yesterday's box is missing; site snapshot every 30 min 7 AM-9:30 PM + 3 min before each tip; grading 11:45 PM and 1:45 PM (10/29 night: the 1:45 PM pass gives the 6-h re-check a run; props and parlay prop legs re-graded every 20+ h for 7 days, up to 8 looks: ESPN keeps correcting boxes days later); backup 12:10 AM (made up at 8:20 AM when that night has no backup file).

## Data

- Box scores / schedule: ESPN via sportsdataverse 2008-now (player boxes 2021-now). Closing lines 2008-2026: `OddsData.sqlite` (cleaned), then the site's own recorded Polymarket closes (`lib/closes.js`).
- Live: Polymarket US (spread / total / moneyline + alternates, 1H spread / total / moneyline, team totals, player props as "N+" ladders — the No side of every NBA player prop is NOT buyable). Books: The Odds API, 7 AM (moneyline + spread + total) and before every tip-off wave (spreads + totals, 2 credits; a 5xx is asked once more) on game days (reference + BOOK GAP).
- stats.nba.com never answers from a data center: no tracking data.

## What the card does

- **PICK side** (the rule with the higher tested % wins; FATIGUE OT 57.8 now outranks BOOK GAP's spread 57.3): LINE MOVE (Pinnacle moved, Polymarket not yet: at the open 67.9%, after 56.3%) > BOOK GAP (books 1-4 off on the spread 57.3% of 171 on Polymarket lines priced 45-55c — the 59.7% was measured on the busiest line and ran 2.4 high; 2-6 on the total 64.1%; measured bought-line rates `BGAP_PTS`; 10/29: only on a books pull from the 90 minutes before the game's tip — live it went 14-18 on pulls up to 6 h old, and the 2025-26 replay with the books' OPENING line loses (45.9 / 47.1%) while the CLOSING line holds on the later half (60.2 / 67.0%)) > FATIGUE (OT back-to-back / 1,500+ km flight) / ATS REGRESSION (away team covering 35% or less of 20+ lined games: 57.3%) / SHOT QUALITY (totals) > REF CREW (totals only, 10/30, `lib/refs.js` + `refs_data.js`: official.nba.com posts each game's crew ~9 AM ET; crew score = sum of its 3 refs' (final total - closing total) / (games + 40) over 2019-2026, 9,346 ESPN-listed crews; top / bottom 20% of crews -> Over / Under, walk-forward 52.4% of 2,304 games 2021-26, 52.8% (624-558) 2024-27, 50-54% every season; all 3 refs need 30+ games; yellow, saved as the model-side total with ruleId `refs|total`, no PICK w/ POINTS, its own Models row; spreads by crew 50-52%, not used; refresh with scratchpad `refs/gen_refs.py`) > the model's side (49.4% spread / 50.4% total, yellow). PICK w/ POINTS = the fewest points from 5 to 10 on the PICK's side that read 72%+ (totals 70%+) priced 82c or less, else the highest % at 82c or less (owner 10/29 late; a rule's lift counts half 5+ points out; spread table from the last 3 seasons + this one, live.py RECENT_SEASONS; BOOK GAP past 3 points reads the larger of its measured 3-point rate and the table + half the lift, 10/29 night); on a model-side spread the team whose 5-10 point line wins more (10/29 night; saved with `alt`, `lib/record.js` keeps it); a row saved before the 72% / 82c rule (no `r72`) is re-saved once before tip-off, the old one kept under removed:); none on the model-side total. MORE = the PICK w/ POINTS line, then only that side's other lines (up to 7 points) at 56%+. A line needs both sides priced 0.1-0.9 (day-early stub markets are never lines, never picks; old stub picks voided).
- **Saved picks** (`lib/record.js recordPicks`, 10/29 night): a tested rule's PICK (cardpick) stands at its first sighting (LINE MOVE is measured at the open); the ML PICK, the model-side PICK and the PICK w/ POINTS follow the card until tip-off (the last snapshot, 3 min before tip, is graded and shown after tip-off; the first sighting stays under `first:<key>`). Until then they were the first sighting, often the day-early line: 10/30's saved MIA -3.5 / HOU -5.5 / BOS -11.5 vs the cards' MIA -7.5 / DAL +5.5 / CHI +13.5, so the card flipped at tip-off. Their tested rates come from closing lines.
- **Model bets (10/30)**: a day the live slate still carries also takes its finished games from the saved picks (the slate drops a game once it is final: 10/29 at 8:19 PM showed MEM @ POR only). A saved PICK w/ POINTS from the old fewest-points rule (no `r72`) leaves the record before tip-off when the 72% / 82c card has none (MEM @ POR 10/29 kept Over 229.5, 3 points).
- **Line table** (`live.py total_rate / spread_rate`): every past game measured against its OWN line. Rule PICKs lift their bought lines by the PICK's edge.
- **ML PICK** = the market's fee-out chance 56%+ (calibrated on 22,003 closes; beats the model).
- **Props**: Overs only, shown % = 0.4 model + 0.6 price (PRA 0.8 model + 0.2 price, `OVER_W_STAT`: real-book PRA Overs priced 65.4c won 78.7% of 357, day-by-day Brier 0.1784 -> 0.1701) then band-calibrated (K 100), 70%+ (owner 10/29; 65% before), priced 60-82c (owner 10/29; 77c from 10/20) and 2+ points under the shown % (PRA exempt), no Over when the player's team is a +2.5 or bigger underdog (47.4% on 2,300 real lines), Threes and rebounds Unders cut. Prop Parlays 6 / 8 / 10 legs per game ($5), Same Game 4 ($5; the card's green PICKs + prop rows); the parlay chance counts same-game links (own team cover + his Over 1.03x, other team 0.97x, total Over + points / assists Over 1.04x).
- **Shadows** (recorded and graded, never picks; off the Models tab since 10/29 late): 1H SPREAD (`lib/h1spread.js`: first-half margin from the full-game line vs every Polymarket 1H spread line, props rules at 56%; real 2025-26 fresh closes 72-48 = 60.0%, fragile), 7 angle mixes (TIRED, BIG DOG vs a tired favorite, RESTED vs star out, RESTED road favorite, RESTED from mid-February, MODEL TOTAL 8+ from March, MODEL TOTAL 8+ under with a star out), LINE MOVE shadows, star out -> teammates' Over, 77c+ hidden prop rows.
- Every game market's prices are saved first + latest at each snapshot (`gmpx:<date>:<game>`).
- **Database (10/28):** from 7:30 PM PT 10/27 the NBA database refused every write silently (every `.set` has a `.catch`), so nothing new was saved while the pages kept reading old data. Models -> System now shows a "Database" row (a test write + memory used / cap; a refused write shows the database's own error) and the watchdog leads with it. Prop copies (~0.7 MB a game day, no expiry) are compacted nightly once a day is graded and 8+ days old (`compactNbaProps`: model copy -> game names, price ladder deleted; graded rows kept).
